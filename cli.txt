COMANDOS Y OPCIONES DEL EJECUTABLE `stxt`
=========================================

--- IMPLEMENTADOS (v0.1.0) ---
stxt --version
stxt --help

--- ENTORNO Y RESOLUCION (v0.2.0) ---
stxt install <file> [--local|--user|--system|--root <dir>]
stxt schemas

--- VALIDACION (v0.3.0) ---
stxt check <path>... [-r|--recursive] [--format json|github]

--- FORMATO (v0.4.0) ---
stxt format <path>... [--tabs|--spaces-4] [--check|--dry-run] [--clean]

--- CONVERSION (v0.5.0) ---
stxt parse <file>
stxt from-json <file>
stxt compile <template>
